title Quick Navigation

actor User
participant Interview Calendar Page
participant Interview Details Modal

User<-Interview Calendar Page: Display all the job interviews for the current month

User->Interview Calendar Page: Click on any of the job interview for the month

Interview Calendar Page->Interview Details Modal: Send selected job interview to display

Interview Details Modal->User: Display the job application (job title and company) the interview is for \nand the interview's date and time 

User->Interview Details Modal: Click 'Done' 

Interview Details Modal->User: Hide job interview details

User->Interview Calendar Page: Redirect to Interview Calendar page
