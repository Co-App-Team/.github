title Deleting a review for a company

actor User
participant Company list
participant Individual Company Modal
participant API

Company list->User:Shows user the \ncurrent company list (all companies)
User->Company list: Clicks on a single company
Individual Company Modal->User: Shows a modal with company information
User->Individual Company Modal: Clicks on a delete button next to \na review, that the user did not write
User<-Individual Company Modal: Indicates to the user that they \ncannot delete this review
User->Individual Company Modal: Clicks on a delete button next to \na review, that the user did write
User<-Individual Company Modal: Displays a confirmation "Are you \nsure you want to delete?" message
User->Individual Company Modal: Clicks "Cancel"
User<-Individual Company Modal: Hides confirmation
User->Individual Company Modal: Clicks on a delete button next to \na review, that the user did write
User<-Individual Company Modal: Displays a confirmation "Are you \nsure you want to delete?" message
User->Individual Company Modal: Clicks "Delete"
Individual Company Modal-->API: DELETE /api/companies/{companyId}/reviews/{reviewId} \nwith review ID in path
User<-Individual Company Modal: Shows a loading spinner
Individual Company Modal-->API: DELETE /api/companies/{companyId}/reviews/{reviewId} \nresponds with success
User<-Individual Company Modal: Replaces spinner with "successfully deleted" message
