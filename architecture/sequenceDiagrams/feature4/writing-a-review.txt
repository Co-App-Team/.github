title Writing a review for a company

actor User
participant Company list
participant Individual Company Modal
participant API

Company list->User:Shows user the \ncurrent company list (all companies)
User->Company list: Clicks on a single company
Individual Company Modal->User: Shows a modal with company information
User->Individual Company Modal: Clicks on "write a review" button
Individual Company Modal->User: Shows a text field input
User->Individual Company Modal: Clicks cancel
Individual Company Modal->User: Hides the text field input
User->Individual Company Modal: Clicks on "write a review" button
Individual Company Modal->User: Shows a text field input
User->Individual Company Modal: Types in the text field input, and clicks "submit"
Individual Company Modal-->API: POST /api/companies/{companyId}/reviews \nwith a payload of the user's text input
User<-Individual Company Modal: Shows a loading spinner
Individual Company Modal<--API: POST /api/companies/{companyId}/reviews \nresponds with success
User<-Individual Company Modal: Replaces spinner with "success" message
