title Creating a company for the Rate My Co-op Screen

actor User
participant Nav Bar
participant New Company Modal
participant API
participant Service
participant MongoDB

Nav Bar -> User: Displays "Rate my Co-op" \nNavigation Item
User -> Nav Bar: Clicks on "Rate my Co-op" \nNavigation Item
User->New Company Modal: Clicks on "Add new company" button
New Company Modal->User: Shows "add new company" modal
New Company Modal<-User: Clicks cancel
New Company Modal->User: Hides "add new company" modal
User->New Company Modal: Clicks on "Add new company" button
New Company Modal->User: Shows "add new company" modal
User->New Company Modal: Inputs Company Name, location,\nand website into the fields
New Company Modal-->API: POST /api/companies/{companyId} \nwith a payload of the user's text input
User<-New Company Modal: Shows a loading spinner
API-->Service: Send company details to backend.\nCompanyService validates information.
Service-->MongoDB: send details to database
MongoDB-->MongoDB: store company information
MongoDB-->Service: send success response
Service-->API: send success response
New Company Modal<--API: POST /api/companies/{companyId} \nresponds with success
User<-New Company Modal: Replaces spinner with "success" message
